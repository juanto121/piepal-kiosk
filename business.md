# PiePal — Business Summary

> **Status:** pre-launch. piepal.co currently shows a "Launching Soon" placeholder (GoDaddy site builder) with an email sign-up form.

## What it is

PiePal sells individual pies through a **self-serve kiosk** in the owner's office. Customers walk up, pick a pie on a touchscreen, pay by scanning a QR code with their phone, and take their pie. Nobody needs to staff it, and it runs on trust, like an honesty box.

## Products & pricing

| Pie | Español | Price | Starting stock |
|---|---|---|---|
| Berries | Pie de frutos rojos | $8.000 COP | 10 |
| Lemon | Pie de limón | $8.000 COP | 10 |

- At most two pie flavors on the menu at any time.
- One pie per purchase.
- Selling all of today's stock brings in **$160.000 COP** (20 pies × $8.000).

## Brand

- **Headline / tagline:** **"La IA no hace pies"** / **"AI doesn't make pies."** Real pies, made by people.
- **Mascot:** a friendly dinosaur who loves pies. It appears on every screen and reacts to what the customer does.
- **Personality:** warm, simple, a little silly.
- **Look:** crust gold and cream, with berry red/purple and lemon yellow as accents; rounded shapes and chunky, friendly type.
- **Language:** Spanish first (Colombian Spanish), with English available.

## Customers & channel

- **Who:** people in and around the owner's office.
- **Where:** one kiosk screen in the office.
- **When:** whenever the office is open. The kiosk runs all day by itself.

## How payment works

- A QR code on the screen links to a fixed **$8.000 COP** payment.
- The customer taps "Ya pagué ✓" after paying. If they don't tap within 2 minutes, the sale still counts but is logged as *unconfirmed* so the owner can check it against the payment app.
- The kiosk never handles money or card details.

## Operations

- **Restocking:** the owner opens a hidden panel on the kiosk (hold the logo for 5 seconds + PIN) to update the counts.
- **Tracking:** the kiosk keeps a simple sales log on the device, each sale marked confirmed or unconfirmed.
- **Sold out:** the kiosk shows it automatically when a flavor reaches 0.
